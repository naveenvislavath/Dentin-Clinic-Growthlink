Privacy Policy

At Dentin Oral Experts, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our patients and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with us online or in person.

1. Information We Collect

  • Full Name

  • Email Address
  • Date of Birth (if required for medical records)
  • Medical history (for treatment purposes)
  • Appointment details
  • Website usage data (via cookies and analytics tools)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Book and manage appointments
